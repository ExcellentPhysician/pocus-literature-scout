A Lung Ultrasound Radiomics-Based Machine Learning Model for Diagnosing Acute Heart Failure in the Emergency Department
2026 · Diagnostics
Builds a radiomics/ML model on lung ultrasound images to diagnose acute heart failure in the ED, reporting AUC 0.976, 90.1% accuracy, 91.1% sensitivity, and 89.1% specificity. Strong reported performance for an automated LUS-based AHF classifier — if it replicates externally, this is a genuine candidate for bedside triage augmentation. Still pre-clinical-deployment stage; worth tracking for validation studies.

Artificial Intelligence–Enhanced Cardiac Point-of-Care Ultrasound: A Prospective Single-Arm Study
2026 · Mayo Clinic Proceedings: Digital Health
A prospective single-arm study of 660 ED/internal-medicine patients scanned with AI-enhanced handheld ultrasound providing automated assessment of ventricular function, valvular disease, pericardial effusion, and IVC size. Adds to the evidence base for AI-augmented handheld cardiac POCUS in non-cardiology hands. Single-arm design (no comparator) limits how strongly conclusions can be drawn.
